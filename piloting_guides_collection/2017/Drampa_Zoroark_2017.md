# How to Pilot — Drampa Zoroark (2017)

_Format: **2017 Standard** (Primal Clash–Burning Shadows, the 2017 season). "The Elusive
Fox" — a Zoroark BREAK / Drampa-GX build. This is the **BKT Zoroark** line (Mind Jack /
Stand In / Foul Play), not Zoroark-GX, which didn't release until Crimson Invasion. Note:
this list still runs **Lysandre** as its gust — the pre-Burning-Shadows configuration (a
Worlds build would usually run Guzma)._

## One-line identity
A consistent, flexible Dark deck: **Zoroark BREAK's Mind Jack** scales with the opponent's
bench and **Foul Play** copies their Active's own attack, while **Drampa-GX's Berserk** hits
a big number off a chip-damaged bench. Zoroark (BKT) keeps you mobile with **Stand In**, and
you punish whatever the opponent presents — your damage comes from *their* board and *their*
attacks.

## The meta it lived in
The 2017 season (PRC-on): Metagross-GX, Vespiquen, Garbodor variants (Espeon/Garb,
Drampa/Garb), Greninja, Volcanion, Vikavolt/Tapu Bulu, and — at Worlds — Gardevoir-GX. This
Zoroark/Drampa was a hyped, versatile pick whose attacks feed off the opponent's own board
and attacks, so it flexes into most matchups. It took **Zoroark BREAK to Top 8 at the 2017
NAIC** (Andrew Mahone). The flip side: it's a "your damage comes from *their* choices" deck,
so opponents who deny it board width or big attacks blunt it.

## Engine cards (why they're here)
- **Zorua (89/162) ×4 → Zoroark (91/162) ×4 → Zoroark BREAK (92/162) ×2** — the core.
  Zoroark **BKT (91)** has **Stand In** (Ability: once per turn, switch this benched Zoroark
  into the Active spot for free) and **Mind Jack** [CC]: **10 + 30 for each of your
  opponent's Benched Pokémon** — up to **160** into a full bench. Zoroark **BREAK** keeps
  both and adds **Foul Play** [D]: **use one of the opponent's Active Pokémon's attacks as
  this attack** — turning their best attacker against them for a single Darkness Energy.
- **Drampa-GX (115/145) ×2** — the muscle, and Colorless so any Energy powers it.
  **Berserk** [CCC]: **80, +70 (→150) if any of *your own* Benched Pokémon have damage
  counters** — and up to **180** vs. a GX/EX with Choice Band. **Righteous Edge** [C] 20:
  discard a **Special Energy** from their Active — great vs. DCE/Rainbow-reliant decks.
  **Big Wheel-GX** shuffles your hand away and draws 10 if you need a full reload.
- **Tapu Lele-GX (60/145) ×3** — Wonder Tag: any Supporter (Brigette, Lysandre, N, Kukui).

## Support / tech (why the odd counts)
- **Eevee (63/98) ×1 → Flareon (13/98) ×1** — a Grass/Fire tech attacker; a specific answer
  (e.g. Vespiquen / evolving decks) that Mind Jack alone can't handle.
- **Sycamore ×4 / N ×3 / Lysandre ×2 / Hex Maniac ×1 / Teammates ×1 / Professor Kukui ×1 /
  Brigette ×1** — draw + reach. **Lysandre** (the gust in this build) drags the KO; **Kukui**
  (+20) pushes Mind Jack / Berserk over exact lines; **Hex Maniac** shuts off Abilities;
  **Teammates** rebuilds after a KO.
- **Ultra Ball ×4 / VS Seeker ×4 / Rescue Stretcher ×2 / Field Blower ×1 / Special Charge
  ×1** — search, recursion (VS Seeker → more Lysandre), recover Zoroark/Drampa, Tool/Stadium
  removal, and **Special Charge** to get DCE back (you lean on it heavily).
- **Choice Band ×3 / Float Stone ×2** — +30 vs. GX/EX for exact KOs; free retreat (Stand In
  covers Zoroark, Float Stone covers Drampa).
- **4 DCE + 4 Rainbow + 5 Darkness** — DCE powers Mind Jack and Drampa cheaply; **Darkness**
  pays Foul Play's Dark cost; **Rainbow** is flexible *and* self-damages the Pokémon you
  attach it to — one counter on your bench turns on **Berserk's +70** for free.

## Ideal early game
- **Turn 1:** **Brigette** (via Tapu Lele) for a full **Zorua** squad + a Drampa-GX; attach
  a Darkness/DCE. A strong Zorua-heavy open is what powers the rest of the game.
- **Turn 2:** evolve Zoroark(s), dig with your draw Supporters (Stand In for mobility), and
  either **Mind Jack** (if their bench is wide) or set up **Berserk**.

## The core loop
Draw and dig with your Zoroark engine, then pick the right attack: **Mind Jack** when their
bench is wide, **Foul Play** to turn their own best attack against them, or **Drampa Berserk**
off a chip-damaged bench. **Righteous Edge** strips Special Energy from Energy-reliant decks.
**Lysandre + Kukui + Choice Band** line up exact KOs. **Stand In** keeps you mobile without
spending your Supporter.

## Priorities / rules of thumb
- **Set up multiple Zoroark** — the deck's consistency and Mind Jack both want a wide board;
  a Brigette turn 1 is huge.
- **Match the attack to the board** — Mind Jack (wide bench), Foul Play (big-attack Active),
  Berserk (chip-damaged bench). Reading which to use is the skill of the deck.
- **Turn on Berserk cheaply** — a Rainbow Energy on your bench is a free damage counter, so
  Drampa's +70 is often already live.
- **Lysandre is your gust** — VS Seeker it; time it (often with Foul Play/Berserk) for the KO
  that matters. (Swap to Guzma if you want the self-pivot the rest of the 2017 field runs.)
- **Manage DCE/Darkness** — Foul Play and Mind Jack lean on Energy; Special Charge and
  careful discarding keep you stocked.

## Weaknesses / bad matchups
- **Fighting** — Zoroark and Zorua are **Fighting-Weak ×2**; a Fighting attacker (Lycanroc-GX)
  trades up on your whole line.
- **Decks that deny board width** — if the opponent keeps a tiny bench, Mind Jack falls off,
  and low-Special-Energy decks blunt Righteous Edge.
- **Metagross-GX / Greninja / big Stage-2 setups** — hard to disrupt; you rely on out-tempoing
  with an early Berserk / Foul Play.
- **Decidueye/Vileplume** — Item lock + stall gives this deck (no lock of its own) trouble.

## Quick reference — the numbers
- Mind Jack: **10 + 30 × opponent's Benched Pokémon** ([CC]) — up to 160 into a full bench.
- Foul Play: **copy the opponent's Active Pokémon's attack** for a single **Darkness** ([D]).
- Berserk (Drampa-GX): **80**, or **150** if any of *your own* Benched Pokémon are damaged;
  up to **180** vs. GX/EX with Choice Band ([CCC]).
- Righteous Edge: **20** + discard a **Special Energy** from their Active ([C]).
- Gust: **Lysandre** in this build; Kukui **+20**, Choice Band **+30 vs. GX/EX**.
