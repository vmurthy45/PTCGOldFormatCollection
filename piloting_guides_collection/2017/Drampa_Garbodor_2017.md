# How to Pilot — Drampa Garbodor (2017)

_Format: **SM-base–Burning Shadows Standard** (the 2017 World Championship format — Burning
Shadows legal, so **Guzma** over Lysandre). The **Zoroark-less** Drampa/Garbodor — Zoroark-GX
didn't release until Crimson Invasion (Nov 2017), so the Worlds build pairs Drampa-GX with a
Trashalanche Garbodor core._

## One-line identity
A single-prize punisher with a GX safety valve: **Garbodor's Trashalanche** does 20 × the
opponent's Items in the discard — a one-shot against the Item-turbo decks that defined the
format — while **Drampa-GX** cleans up as a flexible, no-Energy-type-required attacker.
**Garbotoxin** shuts Abilities off on the side. You give up one Prize per Garbodor and take
two or three back.

## The meta it lived in
2017 Worlds (SM-base–BUS): **Gardevoir-GX** (the eventual winner), **Golisopod/Garbodor**,
**Ho-Oh**, **Volcanion**, and **Garbodor** variants. Every one of those leans on Items
(Ultra Ball, VS Seeker, Float Stone, Rare Candy…) — exactly what Trashalanche feeds on.
Drampa/Garb's pitch was efficiency: a 120-HP non-EX attacker that 1HKOs GXs for a single
Prize, backed by the format's best Ability lock. This is the pre-Zoroark version — the
Drampa-GX partner supplies the reach and disruption that Zoroark BREAK would provide a few
months later.

## Engine cards (why they're here)
- **Trubbish (56/122) ×4 → Garbodor** — the split line, the heart of the deck:
  - **Garbodor (51/145, "Trashalanche") ×3** — **Trashalanche** [P]: **20 × the Items in the
    opponent's discard**. By mid-game that's 6–9 Items → **120–180**; with Choice Band it
    1HKOs almost any GX for **one Prize**. A 120-HP single-prizer that trades up all day.
  - **Garbodor (57/122, "Garbotoxin") ×1** — **Garbotoxin** (needs a Tool attached): every
    Pokémon's Abilities turn off — theirs *and yours*, so sequence around it (below).
- **Drampa-GX (115/145) ×3** — the GX attacker, and Colorless so any Energy powers it.
  **Righteous Edge** [C] 20: discard a **Special Energy** from their Active (wrecks
  Double Colorless / Rainbow decks). **Berserk** [CCC] **80, +70 (→150) if any of *your own*
  Benched Pokémon have damage counters** — so this deck *wants* a lightly-damaged bench (see
  Rainbow Energy). With Choice Band vs. a GX/EX that's up to **180**. **Big Wheel-GX** [C]:
  shuffle your hand away and **draw 10** — a full refuel that wins grind games.
- **Tapu Lele-GX (60/145) ×4** — **Wonder Tag** fetches any Supporter on the turn it's
  benched (Brigette turn 1, Guzma for the KO, N to disrupt). Four copies for consistency, but
  remember Wonder Tag is an Ability — get your searches in **before** Garbotoxin comes down.

## Support / tech (why the odd counts)
- **Sycamore ×3 / N ×4 / Guzma ×2 / Brigette ×1 / Teammates ×1 / Plumeria ×1** — the
  Supporter suite. **Guzma** gusts *and* pivots; **N** disrupts (and shrinks their hand,
  which pairs with the grind); **Teammates** (after a KO) fetches the exact two pieces you
  need; **Plumeria** discards 2 to strip an Energy off their Pokémon — extra disruption on
  top of Righteous Edge.
- **Acerola ×1** — scoop a damaged Garbodor/Drampa (and its Tool/Energy) back to hand: resets
  a hurt attacker, dodges a KO, or re-arms a Garbotoxin body. A flexible one-of.
- **Float Stone ×4 / Choice Band ×4** — **Float Stone** is free retreat *and* the Tool that
  switches **Garbotoxin** on; **Choice Band** (+30 vs. GX/EX) fixes both Trashalanche and
  Berserk onto exact KO numbers. Four each because both jobs come up constantly.
- **VS Seeker ×4 / Ultra Ball ×4 / Field Blower ×2 / Rescue Stretcher ×1 / Super Rod ×1** —
  search and recursion (VS Seeker → more Guzma/N). **Field Blower** clears their Tool/Stadium
  — critically an opposing **Garbotoxin** or a Float Stone holding up *their* Garbotoxin, and
  Choice Bands. **Super Rod / Rescue Stretcher** recover Garbodor line + Energy for the grind.
  (Note: your own Ultra Ball/VS Seeker/Float Stone feed the *opponent's* Trashalanche too — a
  non-issue here, but it's why you don't over-discard.)
- **4 Psychic + 4 Rainbow + 4 Double Colorless** — Psychic powers Trashalanche/Trubbish;
  DCE and Rainbow both cover Drampa's Colorless costs. **Rainbow Energy puts a damage counter
  on the Pokémon you attach it to** — attach one to a benched Pokémon and you've *turned on
  Berserk's +70* for free.

## Ideal early game
- **Turn 1:** **Brigette** (via Tapu Lele) for Trubbish(es) + a Drampa-GX; attach, pass.
- **Early turns:** evolve a **Trashalanche** Garbodor and start swinging as the Item discard
  grows; hold the **Garbotoxin** Garbodor + Float Stone for the turn the lock actually
  matters (and after your own Wonder Tags are done).

## The core loop
Attack with **Trashalanche** — 20 per opposing Item, so it only gets bigger — and trade your
single-prize Garbodor up into their GXs. Bring in **Drampa-GX** for reach: **Righteous Edge**
to strip a Special Energy, **Berserk** (with a chip-damaged bench, +Choice Band) for a big
hit, or **Big Wheel-GX** to reload. Drop **Garbotoxin** to freeze an Ability-reliant deck,
**Guzma** to drag the target, **Plumeria/Righteous Edge** to strip their Energy. **Acerola**
resets a hurt attacker; **N** keeps them low.

## Priorities / rules of thumb
- **Wonder Tag before Garbotoxin.** Garbotoxin turns off *your* Tapu Lele and Acerola-less
  engine too — get your searches done, then attach the Tool to lock.
- **Trashalanche only grows** — you rarely need to rush it; let their Item count climb, and
  use Choice Band for the exact number.
- **Turn on Berserk with Rainbow Energy** — a single damage counter on your bench is +70;
  attach Rainbow to a benched Pokémon when you want the big Drampa hit.
- **Field Blower is a real card in the mirror / vs. Garb** — clear their Garbotoxin (or the
  Float Stone holding it up) to switch your own Abilities back on.
- **Righteous Edge / Plumeria vs. Special-Energy decks** — stripping a DCE off a Gardevoir or
  a Rainbow off a toolbox buys whole turns.

## Weaknesses / bad matchups
- **Low-Item decks** — anything that doesn't lean on Items starves Trashalanche; you fall
  back on Drampa-GX, which is slower.
- **Gardevoir-GX** — high HP, Fairy, and Infinite Force scales with Energy; it grinds well
  and doesn't fold to a single Trashalanche. Righteous Edge on its DCE helps.
- **Fighting (Lycanroc-GX)** — **Drampa-GX is Fighting-Weak ×2**, so a Fighting attacker
  1HKOs your GX partner.
- **Their own Field Blower** — knocking off your Float Stone turns *your* Garbotoxin off and
  can strand a Garbodor with no retreat.

## Quick reference — the numbers
- Trashalanche: **20 × Items in the opponent's discard** ([P]); +30 with Choice Band.
- Berserk: **80**, or **150** if any of **your own** Benched Pokémon are damaged ([CCC]); up
  to **180** vs. GX/EX with Choice Band.
- Righteous Edge: **20** + discard a **Special Energy** from their Active ([C]).
- Big Wheel-GX: shuffle hand away, **draw 10** ([C]).
- Garbotoxin: all Abilities off (both players) **while a Tool is attached** — including yours.
- Drampa-GX: **180 HP**, **Fighting-Weak ×2**; Rainbow Energy self-damages to enable Berserk.
