# How to Pilot — Greninja (2018)

_Format: **2018 Standard** — the Greninja BREAK control deck. (Evosoda saw a promo reprint,
keeping it Standard-legal for the season.)_

## One-line identity
A single-prize control/spread deck. **Giant Water Shuriken** (Greninja BREAK) throws **60
damage at *any* Pokémon every turn** for a Water Energy discarded from hand — position no
object — while **Shadow Stitching** locks the opponent's Abilities. Greninja BREAK isn't a
GX/EX, so it only gives up **one Prize**: you grind the opponent out, healing and recovering
faster than they can trade.

## The meta it lived in
Greninja BREAK was a long-running fixture — a slow, high-skill control deck that punished
Ability- and Special-Energy-reliant decks and out-attritioned fair midrange. It rewards
tight play: the payoff is a single-prize board that heals and recovers faster than most decks
can trade Prizes. The deck's weakness is its own setup: a Stage-2-into-BREAK line is slow, so
raw aggression that races you before the shuriken start flying is the enemy.

## Engine cards (why they're here)
- **Froakie (38/122) ×4 → Frogadier (39/122) ×4 → Greninja (40/122) ×4 → Greninja BREAK
  (41/122) ×3** — the deck. **Frogadier's Water Duplicates** searches **3 Frogadier onto your
  Bench** — the accelerant that builds the whole line fast. **Greninja (BKP 40)** brings the
  two attacks the BREAK keeps via BREAK Evolution: **Shadow Stitching** [C] 40 — until the end
  of the opponent's next turn, *all* their Pokémon (in play, hand, and discard) have **no
  Abilities**; and **Moonlight Slash** [W] 60, or **80** if you return a Water Energy to hand.
- **Greninja BREAK (41/122) ×3** — the damage engine. **Giant Water Shuriken** (Ability, once
  per turn, only while it's your **Active**): discard a Water Energy from hand → **6 damage
  counters (60) on 1 of the opponent's Pokémon, anywhere**. Free-targeting removal that sets
  up KOs the opponent can't see coming. A 170-HP, single-prize body.
- **Staryu (25/122) ×1 → Starmie (31/108, Evolutions) ×1** — **Space Beacon** (Ability):
  discard a card → put **2 basic Energy from your discard back into your hand**. This refuels
  the Water Energy you keep discarding to Giant Water Shuriken — the loop that keeps the
  shuriken flying all game.
- **Splash Energy (113/122) ×1... ×4** — Water Energy that, if its Pokémon is **KO'd by an
  opponent's attack, returns that Pokémon to your hand** instead of the discard. You get your
  Greninja back to redeploy — recovery baked into the Energy.

## Support / tech (why the odd counts)
- **Brooklet Hill (120/145) ×3** — Stadium: search a **Basic Water** Pokémon (Froakie/Staryu)
  to your Bench each turn — free consistency toward the line.
- **Evosoda (116/146) ×4** — searches an **Evolution** that evolves one of your Pokémon in
  play; the extra evolution search that gets Greninja/Frogadier online without Rare Candy.
- **Ultra Ball ×4 / Super Rod ×2 / Rescue Stretcher ×1** — search and recovery; **Super Rod**
  puts Pokémon **and** basic Energy back for the long grind.
- **Enhanced Hammer ×4** — discard a **Special Energy** from an opponent's Pokémon. Four
  copies is a disruption package: it strips Double Colorless / Rainbow / Special Energy decks
  of their Energy while you chip them down.
- **Max Potion ×3** — heal **all** damage from a Greninja (discard all its Energy). Because
  your Greninja attack with a light Energy load (Giant Water Shuriken pays from *hand*, Shadow
  Stitching costs one Colorless), Max Potion is close to a **free full heal** — the core of the
  "they can't KO fast enough" plan.
- **Professor Sycamore ×4 / Cynthia ×4 / N ×4** — heavy draw. **Cynthia** (shuffle-draw 6,
  keeps your hand) is ideal since you often want to *hold* Water Energy for shuriken; **N**
  doubles as late disruption.
- **6 Water + 4 Splash Energy** — Water fuels Moonlight Slash and (from hand) Giant Water
  Shuriken; Splash Energy adds the recover-on-KO safety net. Starmie/Super Rod keep them
  cycling.

## Ideal early game
- **Turn 1:** Froakie(s) down (Brooklet Hill helps), attach, pass; ideally a **Frogadier →
  Water Duplicates** to load the Bench with the line.
- **Turns 2–3:** get a **Greninja BREAK Active**, start **Giant Water Shuriken** (60/turn to
  anything), and drop **Shadow Stitching** on the turns their Abilities matter.

## The core loop
Keep a **Greninja BREAK Active**, **Giant Water Shuriken** a key target for 60 every turn
(refuel the discarded Energy with **Space Beacon**), and swing **Shadow Stitching** to blank
their Abilities or **Moonlight Slash** for 60–80. **Enhanced Hammer** strips their Special
Energy; **Max Potion** wipes damage off a low-Energy Greninja; **Splash Energy / Super Rod /
Rescue Stretcher** bring KO'd Greninja back. You give up one Prize at a time and take theirs
faster.

## Priorities / rules of thumb
- **Water Duplicates early** — a wide Frogadier bench is what makes the whole Stage-2 line
  come together; prioritise it.
- **Hold Energy for shuriken** — Giant Water Shuriken pays from hand, so keep Water Energy in
  hand (Cynthia over Sycamore when you don't want to discard it; Space Beacon to reload).
- **Shadow Stitching on their key turn** — time the Ability lock to blank a Dedenne/engine
  Ability or a heal, not just for the 40.
- **Max Potion is a reset, not a panic button** — with little Energy attached it's a near-free
  full heal; use it to make your Greninja BREAK effectively un-KO-able in a race.
- **Enhanced Hammer the linchpin Energy** — against DCE/Special-Energy decks, stripping the
  attachment can cost them a whole turn.

## Weaknesses / bad matchups
- **Grass (×2 Weakness)** — the entire Water line is **Grass-Weak**; a Grass attacker doubles
  into your Greninja and races the setup.
- **Fast aggro / turn-2 heavy hitters** — a Stage-2-into-BREAK line is slow; decks that apply
  pressure before you're set up are the classic bad matchup.
- **Ability-free, Special-Energy-free decks** — Shadow Stitching does nothing and Enhanced
  Hammer is dead, so half your disruption is off.
- **Big single-shot KOs** — anything that reliably one-shots a 170-HP Greninja BREAK
  out-tempos the Max Potion / Splash Energy recovery plan.

## Quick reference — the numbers
- Giant Water Shuriken: **60 (6 counters) to any Pokémon**, once/turn, Active only, discard a
  Water Energy from hand.
- Shadow Stitching: **40** + opponent's Pokémon have **no Abilities** until end of their next
  turn ([C]).
- Moonlight Slash: **60**, or **80** if you return a Water Energy to hand ([W]).
- Water Duplicates: search **3 Frogadier** to your Bench.
- Space Beacon: discard 1 → **2 basic Energy from discard to hand**.
- Greninja BREAK: **170 HP**, **Grass-Weak ×2**, gives up **1 Prize**.
