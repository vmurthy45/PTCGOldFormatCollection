# How to Pilot — Turbo Rayquaza (2018)

_Format: Sun & Moon-on Standard, the **Celestial Storm** era (the 2018–19 rotation that
opened when CES legalised Rayquaza-GX). A fast, all-in turbo deck that pairs raw damage
output with the format's best natural refuel button._

## One-line identity
Load up **Rayquaza-GX** with basic Grass and Lightning Energy as fast as physically
possible and swing for a scaling **Dragon Break**. Because the attack counts every basic
Grass **and** Lightning Energy attached to *all* of your Pokémon — not just the attacker —
you build a whole bench of charged Rayquaza and hit for 150–210 while **Tempest-GX** gives
you a 10-card refill whenever you stall.

## The meta it lived in
Late-2018 Standard was defined by **Zoroark-GX** variants (ZoroGarb, Zoroark/Lycanroc),
**Buzzwole** Fighting boxes, **Malamar** Psychic engines, and the **Gardevoir/Sylveon**
Fairy decks. Rayquaza was the aggressive, low-interaction "just do damage" option: it
ignores most of the format's Ability tricks and simply races. Its job is to out-tempo the
Zoroark grind and OHKO the format's 180–210-HP GX attackers before they set up.

## How the engine actually works (read this first)
- **Stormy Winds (Ability)** — *when you play Rayquaza-GX from your hand onto your Bench*,
  you may discard the top 3 cards of your deck; if you do, attach a **basic** Energy from
  your **discard pile** to that Rayquaza. This is free acceleration **and** deck-thinning —
  every Rayquaza you bench can arrive pre-charged, provided there's a basic Energy already
  in the discard.
- **Dragon Break** `[G][L][C]` — **30 × the number of basic Grass + basic Lightning Energy
  attached to *your Pokémon*** (all of them, board-wide). Two energy on the attacker + two
  on each of two benched Rayquaza = 6 energy = **180**; add **Choice Band** for **210** vs.
  a GX/EX. This is why you spread energy across the bench instead of hoarding it on one.
- **Tempest-GX** `[G]` — discard your hand and draw **10** cards. Your one-time "I flooded /
  I got N'd to 1 / I need everything" reset. Usually taken mid-game, not turn 1.

## Engine cards (why they're here)
- **Rayquaza-GX (109/168) ×4** — the whole deck. 180 HP Dragon attacker; you want 2–3 in
  play, each holding energy, to push Dragon Break's number up.
- **Marshadow (SLG 45) ×2** — **Let Loose**: on bench, both players shuffle hand into deck
  and draw 4. Doubles as a disruption button (wreck the opponent's built-up hand) and a
  personal reset. Note the ability triggers **when you bench it from hand**.
- **Tapu Lele-GX (60/145) ×2** — **Wonder Tag**: search any Supporter (grab **Sycamore**,
  **Guzma**, or **Lillie** exactly when you need it). The turn-1 consistency glue.
- **Oranguru (SUM 113) ×1** — **Instruct**: once per turn draw until you have 3 in hand — a
  no-Supporter top-up that keeps the engine running after you've dumped your hand.

## Support / tech (why the counts)
- **Max Elixir ×4** — attach a basic Energy from the top 5 of your deck to a benched Basic.
  With 14 basic Energy in the list the hit rate is high; this is your primary off-turn
  accelerant onto benched Rayquaza. (Only hits **benched** Basics — plan attachments so a
  fresh Rayquaza is on the bench for it.)
- **Mysterious Treasure ×4** — discard a card → search any **Dragon or Psychic** Pokémon
  (Rayquaza is Dragon; Tapu Lele/Marshadow are Psychic). The discard cost is a *feature*: it
  seeds a basic Energy into the discard for **Stormy Winds** to grab.
- **Acro Bike ×3** — draw 2, keep 1, discard 1. Dig speed **and** more discard-fuel for
  Stormy Winds.
- **Ultra Ball ×2** — any Pokémon; more discard fuel; grabs Tapu Lele/Oranguru.
- **Professor Sycamore ×4 / Lillie ×1** — raw draw. **Sycamore** discards your hand (fine —
  it fills the discard with energy for Stormy Winds); **Lillie** is a strong turn-1 draw-to-8.
- **Guzma ×4** — gust up a benched threat / drag a wall out of the way and pivot in a
  charged Rayquaza the same turn. Four copies because Dragon Break rewards picking the KO.
- **Puzzle of Time ×4** — play 2 together to retrieve **any 2** cards from the discard: loop
  a KO'd Rayquaza, a Guzma, an Energy, or a used Item. The recursion that keeps an all-in
  deck from running out of gas.
- **Rescue Stretcher ×2 / Super Rod ×1** — recover Rayquaza (Stretcher) and shuffle back
  Pokémon **and Energy** (Super Rod) so Dragon Break's counter doesn't run dry late.
- **Field Blower ×2** — remove opposing Tools/Stadiums (Garbotoxin Tool, Choice Band, damage
  stadiums) that would otherwise blank your turn.
- **Choice Band ×3** — +30 vs. GX/EX: the difference between 180 and a **210** OHKO on the
  format's big GX.
- **Float Stone ×3 / Escape Rope ×1** — Rayquaza's retreat is **3**; Float Stone gives a
  charged Rayquaza free retreat, and Escape Rope pivots a fresh attacker into the Active
  (and can disrupt the opponent's board).
- **7 Grass + 7 Lightning basic Energy** — both count for Dragon Break, so the split is
  deliberate; you almost never care *which* colour lands, only the **total** in play.

## Ideal early game
- **Turn 1:** Tapu Lele → **Lillie** or **Sycamore** to dig; get **Rayquaza on the bench**,
  fire **Stormy Winds** (seed a basic Energy in the discard first via Treasure/Acro Bike so
  it can attach), and set up **Max Elixir** targets. Manual-attach to a benched Rayquaza.
- **Turn 2:** bench a 2nd (ideally 3rd) Rayquaza — each Stormy Winds arrival adds energy —
  chain **Max Elixir**, then either promote a charged Rayquaza (Float Stone/Escape Rope) or
  **Guzma** something up and **Dragon Break** for the first KO.

## The core loop
Every turn: draw hard (Sycamore/Lillie/Oranguru), bench another Rayquaza to trigger Stormy
Winds, pile basic Energy onto the bench with **Max Elixir** + manual attach, then **Guzma +
Dragon Break** the biggest threat. Keep the board-wide energy count climbing so the number
only goes up. **Tempest-GX** once when you flood or get disrupted; **Puzzle of Time** to buy
back whatever the opponent KO'd.

## Priorities / rules of thumb
- **Spread energy, don't stack it.** Dragon Break counts the whole board — two Rayquaza with
  3 each out-damages one with 4, and it survives a single KO with damage still online.
- **Seed the discard before Stormy Winds.** Play Mysterious Treasure / Acro Bike / Sycamore
  *first* so there's a basic Energy waiting for the ability to attach.
- **Count to the OHKO.** 5 energy = 150, 6 = 180, +Choice Band = 180/210 vs GX/EX. Know the
  opponent's HP and add exactly enough before you swing.
- **Hold Tempest-GX** until it actually saves the game (post-N, post-flood). It's your only
  GX attack.
- **Field Blower on curve** vs. Garbotoxin (which would switch **Stormy Winds** off) and vs.
  their Choice Band/Stadium.

## Weaknesses / bad matchups
- **Fairy Weakness (×2).** **Gardevoir-GX** (your own sibling deck, Zoroark/Gardevoir) and
  **Alolan Ninetales-GX** (Mysterious Treasure copying + Fairy) OHKO Rayquaza back for cheap.
  This is the scariest wall in the format.
- **N / Marshadow to a low hand** hurts an all-in draw deck; you lean on Oranguru, Tapu Lele,
  and your own Tempest-GX to recover.
- **Ability lock (Garbotoxin) / heavy disruption** slows Stormy Winds and Max Elixir setup —
  Field Blower is mandatory there.
- **You KO yourself into trouble if you over-invest** — a single Guzma + big hit onto your
  charged attacker drops your board-wide count; keep a backup Rayquaza charged.

## Quick reference — the numbers
- **9 Pokémon** — 4 Rayquaza-GX, 2 Marshadow, 2 Tapu Lele-GX, 1 Oranguru.
- **37 Trainers** — 4 Sycamore, 1 Lillie, 4 Guzma, 4 Puzzle of Time, 4 Mysterious Treasure,
  4 Max Elixir, 3 Acro Bike, 2 Ultra Ball, 2 Rescue Stretcher, 1 Super Rod, 1 Escape Rope,
  2 Field Blower, 3 Choice Band, 2 Float Stone.
- **14 Energy** — 7 Grass, 7 Lightning (both feed Dragon Break).
- **Dragon Break math:** 30 × (all basic G + L in play). 4→120, 5→150, 6→180; **+30** with
  Choice Band vs. GX/EX. **Weakness Fairy ×2, retreat 3, 180 HP.**
