# How to Pilot — Eternatus (2021)

_Format: Sword & Shield Standard (Battle Styles → Chilling Reign era). The premier
**Darkness turbo-VMAX** deck: fill an eight-Pokémon Bench with Darkness types and attack
for a damage number that scales with your own board._

## One-line identity
**Eternatus VMAX's Dread End does 30 damage for every Darkness Pokémon you have in play.**
Its **Eternal Zone** Ability lets you run a **Bench of 8** (as long as every Pokémon is
Darkness), so a full board of nine Darkness Pokémon swings for **up to 270** — for just
`[D][C]`. It's the fastest, most consistent aggro deck in the format: dump the board turn 1,
evolve, and start one-shotting by turn 2.

## The engine (how the damage happens)
- **Eternatus VMAX (117/189) ×4** — 340 HP, Darkness.
  - **Eternal Zone** (Ability): *if all your Pokémon in play are Darkness type, you may have up
    to **8 on your Bench**, and you can't play non-Darkness Pokémon.* This is the whole deck's
    permission slip — it turns "number of Darkness Pokémon" into a big number.
  - **Dread End** `[D][C]`: **30 × (your Darkness Pokémon in play)**. Active + 8 Bench = 9 → **270**.
    Realistically you're swinging for **210–270** from turn 2 on, for two Energy.
- **Eternatus V (116/189) ×4** — 220 HP. **Power Accelerator** `[C]` 30: *attach a Darkness Energy
  from hand to a Benched Pokémon* — free acceleration while you develop. **Dynamax Cannon**
  `[D][C][C][C]` 120 (**+120 vs a VMAX**) is a relevant backup vs the mirror and other VMAXs.
- **Crobat V (104/189) ×4** — **Dark Asset**: when played from hand, **draw until you have 6**.
  Your consistency turbo *and* a Darkness body that pads Dread End. (One Dark Asset per turn.)
- **Galarian Zigzagoon (117/202) ×4** — **Headbutt Tantrum**: when played from hand, **put 1
  damage counter on an opponent's Pokémon**. Free chip that pushes targets into Dread End KO
  range and softens future prizes — and it's another Darkness body on the Bench.

## Bench-filler tech (all Darkness, so they all count for Dread End)
- **Galarian Moltres V (97/198) ×1** — **Direflame Wings**: once per turn, **attach a Darkness
  Energy from your discard**. A second attacker (**Aura Burn** `[D][D][C]` 190, 30 to itself) and
  an Energy-recursion engine that reloads after the discard piles up.
- **Liepard V (104/198) ×1** — **Hidden Claw**: when played from hand, **discard a Pokémon Tool**
  from any Pokémon. Free tool removal (opposing Big Charm, Rapid Strike Scroll, etc.), and
  **Shadow Ripper** `[D][C][C]` 110 can bounce Liepard back to hand to re-use Hidden Claw.

## Support / consistency (why it never bricks)
- **Professor's Research (178/202) ×4 · Marnie (169/202) ×4** — eight draw Supporters; Marnie also
  disrupts the opponent's hand while you set up.
- **Quick Ball (179/202) ×4 · Great Ball (164/202) ×3 · Pokémon Communication (152/181) ×4** — a
  huge ball count to find Crobat V, the Zigzagoons, and the Eternatus line turn 1.
- **Boss's Orders (154/192) ×3** — gust the piece you need to KO (or the thing hiding from Dread
  End) to close games.
- **Switch (183/202) ×4** — Eternatus VMAX **retreats for 3**, so Switch keeps your attacker free
  and lets you pivot a damaged VMAX out.
- **Energy Switch (162/202) ×3** — move Darkness Energy onto the attacker so a fresh VMAX can
  Dread End the turn it evolves (pairs with Power Accelerator's bench attachments).
- **Big Charm (158/202) ×2** — Tool: **+50 HP**. Eternatus VMAX at **390 HP** climbs out of a lot
  of one-shot ranges.
- **Chaotic Swell (187/236) ×2** — Stadium: **whenever anyone plays a Stadium, discard it** (and
  this). Your answer to **Path to the Peak** — the card that would switch off Eternal Zone (and
  collapse your Bench to 5) and turn off Crobat V/Moltres. Keeping the opponent's Stadium off the
  board is often the whole game.
- **Energy: 9 basic Darkness.** Dread End only needs `[D][C]`, and Power Accelerator / Direflame
  Wings recur attachments, so the count stays low.

## Ideal early game
- **Turn 1:** Ball out a big **Darkness** Bench — **Crobat V (draw to 6)**, **Galarian Zigzagoon
  ×N (free damage counters)**, an **Eternatus V**, maybe Moltres/Liepard. Every body you drop
  both **grows Dread End** and triggers an on-play Ability. Attach, **Power Accelerator** to seed
  a benched Pokémon.
- **Turn 2:** evolve **Eternatus V → VMAX**, make sure **all your Pokémon are Darkness** so
  Eternal Zone is live and the Bench is legal at 8, get to `[D][C]`, and **Dread End** for
  210–270. Use Zigzagoon counters + Boss's Orders to convert that into the exact KO you want.

## The core loop
Keep the **Bench full of Darkness bodies** (that's your damage), attack with **Dread End** every
turn, and use **Headbutt Tantrum + Boss's Orders** to snipe the right target. **Big Charm** and
340/390 HP mean you usually survive a hit and swing back; **Chaotic Swell** keeps Path to the Peak
from ever landing. When the board thins, **re-fill the Bench** — a rebuilt board is a rebuilt
damage number.

## Priorities / rules of thumb
- **Keep every Pokémon Darkness.** The instant a non-Darkness sneaks in (or Eternal Zone gets
  shut off), your Bench collapses to 5 and Dread End craters. Don't play non-Darkness Pokémon.
- **Bench = damage.** Before you attack, count your Darkness Pokémon and make sure you're at the
  number you need — dropping one more Crobat/Zigzagoon can be the extra 30 that KOs.
- **Hold Chaotic Swell for Path to the Peak**, and don't be shy about replaying it — stadium
  denial is your best matchup insurance.
- **Manage the retreat.** Switch out a low VMAX rather than paying 3; keep a healthy attacker
  Active.
- **Sequence on-play Abilities** (Crobat, Zigzagoon, Liepard, Moltres) before you commit to the
  attack — you often want the extra draw or damage counter *first*.

## Matchups
- **Beats the slower VMAX/box decks** on raw speed and consistency — you're attacking for 240+ a
  turn before many decks are set up, and Marnie/Boss's keep them off balance.
- **Path-to-the-Peak decks** are the skill matchup: race hard, lean on **Chaotic Swell**, and
  don't over-commit Rule-Box Pokémon you can't afford to lose to a shut-off Bench.
- **Spread/single-prize decks** can be awkward — they don't mind trading small Pokémon while you
  feed them prizes on your V/VMAXs.

## Weaknesses / bad matchups
- **Fighting, hard.** Eternatus V and VMAX are **Fighting Weakness ×2** — **Single Strike
  Urshifu VMAX** and other Fighting hitters one-shot your board for cheap. This is the deck's
  defining bad matchup; you have to out-tempo it and gust its setup before it's online.
- **Path to the Peak** (if it sticks) turns off Eternal Zone → Bench to 5, and kills Crobat/Moltres
  — Chaotic Swell is the only real answer, so drawing it matters.
- **Giving up two-prize V/VMAXs** — Crobat V, the Eternatus line, Moltres/Liepard are all
  multi-prize, so decks that snipe your Bench V's (or race single-prizers) can prize you out.

## Quick reference — the numbers
- **18 Pokémon** — 4 Eternatus V, 4 Eternatus VMAX, 4 Crobat V, 4 Galarian Zigzagoon,
  1 Galarian Moltres V, 1 Liepard V (all Darkness — that's the point).
- **33 Trainers** — 4 Professor's Research, 4 Marnie, 4 Quick Ball, 4 Switch, 4 Pokémon
  Communication, 3 Boss's Orders, 3 Great Ball, 3 Energy Switch, 2 Big Charm, 2 Chaotic Swell.
- **9 Energy** — 9 basic Darkness.
- **Dread End:** `[D][C]`, **30 × your Darkness Pokémon in play** (up to ~270 on a full board).
  Eternatus VMAX is **340 HP → 390** under Big Charm; **Fighting Weakness ×2**.
